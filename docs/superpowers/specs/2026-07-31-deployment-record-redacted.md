# Site deployment design removed

The former deployment design described a real installation and was removed from the tracked tree
under [Decisions Register R61](../../decisions-register.md#r61--site-specific-operational-records-never-enter-this-repository--2026-08-02).
A pseudonymized deployment dossier is still a dossier: topology, inventories, access posture,
backup design, and accepted weaknesses can identify and endanger the organization even when names
and addresses are replaced.

Keep only reusable product lessons in generic runbooks or design records. The organization's
concrete design and risk acceptance belong in its own controlled operational documentation.
